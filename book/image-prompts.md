# VESPER — Image Prompts
*Visual reference prompts for key locations, characters, and atmosphere.*

---

## ELATHON — The Hometown (Before the Rot)

**Prompt 1 — Town Overview**
```
A small ancient Mediterranean hill town built on layered ruins, warm golden stone 
architecture, Byzantine influences, terracotta rooftops, narrow winding streets, 
olive groves cascading down the hillside into a sun-bleached valley below, old 
crumbling walls visible beneath newer buildings, late afternoon light casting long 
shadows, the sea faintly visible at the horizon, lush green olive trees covering 
the lower slopes, a sense of deep antiquity and quiet pride, photorealistic 
fantasy art, muted warm palette, slightly melancholic beauty
```

**Prompt 2 — The Lower Olive Grove (Healthy)**
```
Ancient olive grove on a Mediterranean hillside, gnarled silver-grey olive trees 
thousands of years old, twisted trunks as wide as a person's armspan, dappled 
afternoon sunlight through dense silver-green leaves, dry golden grass beneath 
the trees, old stone walls dividing the grove into sections, the trees so old they 
predate any building visible, a sense of living memory, timeless and still, warm 
Mediterranean light, photorealistic fantasy art, muted earth tones, deep greens 
and ochres
```

## ELATHON — The Hometown (With the Rot)

**Prompt 3 — Town with the Rot Beginning**
```
A small ancient Mediterranean hill town in slow decay, Byzantine stone 
architecture, the lower hillside olive groves turned ashen and grey, dead trees 
with blackened bark among living ones, the blight spreading in a spiral pattern 
outward from the valley floor, upper groves still faintly green, the town itself 
intact but diminished, streets quieter than they should be, a grey-green discoloration 
creeping at the edges of walls and stone, overcast light replacing the usual 
southern gold, grimdark fantasy art, desaturated palette, melancholic atmosphere, 
dying beauty
```

**Prompt 4 — The Lower Olive Grove (Dying)**
```
Ancient dying olive grove, gnarled thousand-year-old olive trees with ashen bark 
and grey withered leaves, the ground beneath them cracked and pale, a strange 
dusty discoloration spreading across the soil, some trees completely bare, others 
mid-decay, the death spreading outward in a slow spiral, no insects, no birdsong 
implied, an uncanny wrongness to the stillness, the light flat and grey where it 
was once gold, a few stones and old walls visible, grimdark fantasy art, 
desaturated greens and greys, atmosphere of irreversible loss
```

**Prompt 5 — A Fully Blighted Region (What Elathon Could Become)**
```
A devastated Mediterranean landscape, ruins of a once-prosperous town visible 
through dead grey trees, no living vegetation, the soil pale and cracked like old 
ash, dead olive groves covering the hillsides, the ruins of stone buildings 
half-consumed by the blight, the sky a flat overcast white, no color except shades 
of grey and bone, the remnants of an aqueduct visible in the background, 
overgrown with something dark and wrong, grimdark fantasy art, almost monochrome 
palette, atmosphere of irreversible civilizational loss, the beauty of ruin
```

## KESSARA — The Imperial City

**Prompt 6 — Kessara Overview**
```
A large Byzantine-inspired Mediterranean city built on a rocky coastline, grand 
stone architecture layered over centuries of older buildings, aqueducts visible 
crossing the hillsides above the city, a harbor under active construction with 
cranes and scaffolding, markets and wide streets in the lower city, domed rooftops 
and columned facades, the city grand but subtly diminished, the sea visible beyond 
the harbor slightly brackish and grey where it meets the docks, late afternoon 
light, photorealistic grimdark fantasy art, warm stone tones fading toward grey 
at the edges, a city that believes in itself despite the evidence
```

**Prompt 7 — The Archive Building**
```
Interior of an ancient archive in a converted Byzantine temple, three floors of 
stone shelves holding codices and scrolls, narrow tall windows letting in shafts 
of afternoon light, wooden reading tables with oil lamps burning, the architecture 
of an older temple visible beneath later renovations — old columns incorporated 
into new walls, carved stonework half-plastered over, the smell of old parchment 
implied in the dimness, dust motes in the lamplight, a scholar's desk covered in 
open documents, a locked wooden chest beneath the desk, photorealistic fantasy art, 
warm amber lamplight against cool grey stone, atmosphere of contained order
```

## VESPER

**Prompt 8 — Vesper at Work**
```
A woman in her late thirties, dark hair simply arranged, wearing practical dark 
scholar's robes, seated at a wooden desk in a lamplit archive, leaning over an 
open codex with a pen in hand, three oil lamps burning beside her, her expression 
one of total concentration, the slight shadows under her eyes suggesting this is 
a habit not an occasion, bookshelves and scrolls behind her, a locked wooden 
chest visible beneath the desk, Byzantine-influenced clothing, photorealistic 
fantasy portrait, warm lamplight, the face of someone who has learned to live 
very quietly inside her own mind
```

**Prompt 9 — Vesper on the Road South**
```
A woman traveling alone on an ancient Roman-style road through a southern 
Mediterranean landscape, practical travel clothing over scholar's robes, a bag 
over one shoulder, the road stretching ahead through olive groves and dry hills, 
her posture slightly reluctant — walking forward while looking as though part of 
her is still at her desk, the landscape beautiful and familiar and slightly wrong 
at the edges, some trees in the distance beginning to show the grey of the blight, 
late afternoon light, photorealistic grimdark fantasy art, warm palette cooling 
toward the horizon
```

## THE EMPIRE OF AETHERIS

**Prompt 10 — The Empire at Its Height (Historical Reference)**
```
A grand Byzantine-Mediterranean empire at its peak, sweeping aqueducts crossing 
mountain valleys, a vast city visible on the coast, roads cutting through lush 
southern landscape, olive groves and vineyards on every hillside, monuments and 
columns visible on the hilltops, ships in a busy harbor, the sky gold and clear, 
an empire that believes it has mastered the world, photorealistic fantasy art, 
rich warm palette, monumental and beautiful, the kind of civilization that names 
itself after the sky
```

**Prompt 11 — The Empire Now (Fading)**
```
A fading Byzantine-Mediterranean empire, the same grand aqueducts now carrying 
slightly brackish water, the great city smaller than its walls suggest it should 
be, scaffolding on half-finished restoration projects, some vineyards abandoned 
and grey, the roads still intact but quieter, the harbor still grand but the 
catch smaller, the monuments still standing but the gold light replaced by 
something flatter, the empire still calling itself by the same name, not yet 
admitting what the edges of the map already show, photorealistic grimdark fantasy 
art, warm palette desaturating toward grey at the margins
```

## THE ROT ITSELF

**Prompt 12 — The Rot Up Close**
```
Close-up of the blight on an ancient olive tree, the bark going pale and ashen 
from the roots upward, a strange dusty grey discoloration spreading through the 
wood grain, the leaves beginning to silver and curl, the soil around the base 
pale and cracked, no insects on the bark, the wrongness subtle but undeniable — 
not fire, not disease, something slower and more total, photorealistic fantasy 
art, desaturated natural tones, the kind of image that makes the viewer feel 
the silence
```

**Prompt 13 — The Rot's Spiral Pattern (Aerial View)**
```
Aerial view of a Mediterranean valley showing the blight's spiral spread, green 
landscape at the edges fading to grey and ash toward the center, the pattern 
disturbingly geometric — not random but radiating outward in slow spirals from 
the most settled and built-up areas, ruins of attempted interventions visible 
as darker patches within the grey, the living green at the edges pressing inward 
as the grey presses outward, grimdark fantasy concept art, bird's-eye perspective, 
the pattern legible as something systemic rather than natural
```
