## AMAZON BOOK DESCRIPTION

For eleven years, environmental historian Vesper has been building the same argument: that every human effort to contain a catastrophe generates a larger catastrophe in response, and that this pattern is not a flaw in our institutions but the oldest feature of our species.

Now the argument is finished. The eleven pages — a document too complete to ignore and too dangerous to publish — have been sent to a journal in London. And Vesper is on a train going south, back to the village she left twenty-two years ago: to a grove of olive trees dying from the same programs she spent her career studying, to the woman who kept the house lit while she was gone, and to the work of a man who stayed when she couldn't.

She has spent five months gathering the final evidence — speaking with a researcher in Beirut who has rebuilt her city four times and kept her sense of humor through all of it; with farmers blocking French highways with their tractors; with survivors of American nuclear tests who have been testifying before committees for forty years; with an eighty-seven-year-old woman tending her mother's sunflowers inside the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone. Each of them told her, in different languages and different registers, the same thing the data cannot say.

*The Interval* is a novel about the space between catastrophes — what grows there, what waits there, and what it costs to finally come home to it.

## FORMAT CONVERSION NOTE

KDP accepts .docx directly. To convert from the compiled markdown:

1. Open `manuscript-complete.md` in a text editor
2. Use Pandoc (free tool) to convert: `pandoc manuscript-complete.md -o manuscript.docx`
3. In the Word document, set:
   - Body font: Garamond or Georgia, 11pt
   - Line spacing: 1.15
   - Chapter headings: centered, 14pt
   - Section breaks (---): centered ornament or just space
   - Italics (flashback sections): already marked with * in the markdown, will convert automatically
4. Upload the .docx to KDP

*Alternatively, KDP's online formatter can handle a clean .docx directly.*
