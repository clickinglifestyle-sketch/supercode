# THE INTERVAL — Cover Design Brief
## For designer or AI image generation

---

## THE BOOK IN ONE SENTENCE

A literary novel about an environmental historian who has spent eleven years documenting the end of the world, and who finally comes home to a dying olive grove in Greece.

---

## TONE AND FEEL

- **Quiet, serious, beautiful.** Not commercial or genre. Think literary fiction published by Farrar, Straus and Giroux or Canongate.
- **Melancholy but not hopeless.** There is grief in the book but also presence, attention, something that endures.
- **European literary sensibility.** The book moves through Beirut, France, Berlin, Japan, the American Southwest, and Chernobyl — but its heart is a Greek hillside.
- **Comparables:** *The Remains of the Day* (Ishiguro), *A Field Guide to Getting Lost* (Solnit), *The Rings of Saturn* (Sebald). Spare, intelligent covers.

---

## VISUAL CONCEPTS

### Concept A — THE TREE (Recommended)
**Close-up or medium shot of ancient olive tree bark.**

- The bark of a very old olive tree: deeply fissured, grey-brown, with living texture — compressed and patient. The tree is alive but marked by time.
- Light source: low and lateral, late afternoon Mediterranean sun. One side warmly lit, the other in shadow.
- The image fills most of the cover. The title is placed either overlaid (in a clean serif font, possibly reversed out in white or pale cream) or below the image on a plain background.
- The bark should feel real and specific — not decorative. This is a tree that has been standing for centuries and will outlast the people in the book.

### Concept B — THE GROVE
**A woman, seen from behind or at distance, standing among olive trees.**

- Mediterranean hillside. Some trees are alive — grey-green leaves, twisted trunks. Some are bare, clearly dead — the grey of something that has stopped rather than the grey of winter.
- The woman is small relative to the trees. She is present, still, not moving away.
- Light: late afternoon, golden, the kind that makes everything look like a memory.
- Color palette: grey, olive green, pale gold, soft white.

### Concept C — MINIMAL TYPOGRAPHY
**No image. Pure typography on a textured background.**

- Title: THE INTERVAL in very large, widely spaced serif capitals
- Author: William Gomez in smaller type below
- Background: a subtle texture suggesting soil or bark or paper — not plain white
- This works if Concepts A or B prove difficult to execute well

## COLOR PALETTE (all concepts)

| Color | Use |
|-------|-----|
| Warm grey (#8C8C7A) | Dead/dying trees, bark |
| Olive green (#6B7045) | Living foliage |
| Pale gold (#C9A84C) | Light, warmth |
| Off-white / cream (#F5F0E8) | Text, negative space |
| Deep charcoal (#2C2C2C) | Title text |

Avoid: bright colors, blue tones, anything that reads as genre (thriller black, romance pink, sci-fi metallic).

---

## TYPOGRAPHY

- **Title font:** Clean, elegant serif. Suggestions: Cormorant Garamond, Freight Display, Caslon, or EB Garamond. Set with generous letter-spacing.
- **Title layout:** THE INTERVAL on one or two lines. "THE" smaller or lighter than "INTERVAL" if desired.
- **Author name:** William Gomez — smaller, beneath title, same font family.
- **No taglines or quotes on the front cover.**

## DESIGN NOTES

- **No bright colors.** The book is serious and quiet.
- **The title should be immediately readable** at thumbnail size (Amazon displays covers very small in search results — test legibility at 100px width).
- **Leave room for the title.** If using the bark image, the title can be placed in a slightly darker or lighter area of the bark texture.
- **No stock photo clichés:** no globes, no hands reaching, no generic "environment" imagery (windmills, solar panels). This is a literary novel, not a climate report.
