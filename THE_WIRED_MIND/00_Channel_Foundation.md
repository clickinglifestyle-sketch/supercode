## Visual Identity System

### Art Style
2D flat cartoon animation with clean bold line art. Warm, editorial aesthetic. Kurzgesagt-style simplicity meets editorial illustration warmth.

### Signature Character Design
- Perfectly round white oval head
- Simple dot eyes, calm/curious expression
- Minimal mouth (often no mouth visible)
- Spiky dark hair
- Bright yellow hoodie
- Dark pants, simple sneakers
- Deliberately generic — universal viewer projection

### Color Palette

| Role | Color |
|------|-------|
| Primary | Bright yellow (character hoodie — signature anchor) |
| Secondary | Deep navy / midnight blue (night, thought, weight) |
| Tertiary | Warm amber / beige (indoor, lamplight, café) |
| Accent | Soft gold highlights, muted whites |

### Lighting
Always soft and atmospheric. Warm interior glows. No harsh shadows. Gentle gradients. Ambient haze for depth.

### Camera Composition
- Centered medium full-body shots (default)
- Side-profile observational shots
- Wide environmental establishing shots
- Thought-bubble comic-panel sequences as visual metaphors
