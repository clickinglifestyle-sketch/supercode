# 1M Template Brief

## What It Is
The high-potential video structure for topics that can realistically reach 1M+ views. Used when a topic passes all five criteria in the filter below.

## 1M Criteria Filter (All 5 Must Pass)

1. Behavior practiced by a significant minority
2. Majority quietly judges or misunderstands it
3. Can be reframed from "odd/antisocial" → "psychologically evolved"
4. Creates double audience (the person + people who know that person)
5. Connects to a current cultural tension

## Script Architecture (10 Beats)

| # | Beat | Notes |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | Universal mental image — viewer sees themselves immediately |
| 2 | Surface assumption | Name the common misread of the behavior |
| 3 | Curiosity signal | Tease that there's more going on beneath the surface |
| 4 | Psychological reframe | Flip the assumption — this is actually about X |
| 5 | Profile 1 | Healthy boundary-setter — grounding, legitimate |
| 6 | Profile 2 | Anxious/vulnerable — creates empathy, broadens audience |
| 7 | Profile 3 | Psychologically evolved — aspirational, reward for watching |
| 8 | Research block | Named study + institution — builds credibility |
| 9 | Cultural observation | Situates the behavior in the current moment |
| 10 | Quotable closer | Standalone shareable object — the takeaway that gets screenshotted |

## Runtime
6:30–7:00 (~870 words at ~130 wpm)

## Why 3 Profiles Instead of 7 Signs
The 3-profile structure creates empathy across a wider audience spectrum. Viewers who don't fully identify with the behavior can still find their entry point — either through the anxious type (Profile 2) or through someone they know. This is what creates the double audience and drives shares.
